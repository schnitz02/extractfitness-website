About Extract

Built by athletes,
for athletes.

Extract Fitness isn't your average gym. Founded by Tom in late 2025, it was built on a simple idea: every session should have a purpose. No aimless reps. No cookie-cutter programs.

We're a coaching-first environment specialising in strength & conditioning. Whether you're a competitive athlete or someone who wants to train like one, Extract is where you come to get serious about performance.

Featured Coach

Noah Barak

"Trained at semi-pro level. Now I help others move like it."

Noah brings 10+ years of competitive soccer experience to his coaching. A former NPL1 striker with South Melbourne FC — one level below A-League — he understands what it takes to perform at the elite level.

His approach goes beyond aesthetics. Every session is built around explosive movement, athletic conditioning, and injury-resilient training. Whether you're a competitive footballer, weekend warrior, or someone who wants to move with power and purpose — Noah builds programs that deliver.
